What Makes It Different

Built for people who want
beauty and function

01

Color-Coded Glow

Each reminder gets its own color. Purple for medication. Blue for meetings. Green for movement. You learn the language of light — your phone stays in your pocket.

02

Interchangeable Resin Faces

Every resin face is hand-poured and one of a kind. Swap your face in seconds to match your outfit, mood, or occasion. Collect them. Gift them. Make them yours.

03

No App Required

Plug in the magnetic cable, open Chrome, and set your reminders in seconds. No app to download. No account to create. Unplug and wear.

A soft pulse on your wrist — never disruptive, never ignored. The bracelet remembers your schedule even when your phone is off. Double-tap the face to dismiss.

No screen. No digital display. No fitness tracker aesthetic. Luminary Jewels is designed to be worn to dinner, to meetings, and to bed — because beautiful things should work hard.

For women building routines. For teens learning independence. For mothers who want their kids off their phones. One bracelet, endless uses.

Simple by Design

Set it once.
Wear it always.

01

Snap the Cable

The magnetic pogo cable attaches to the underside of your bracelet. No ports. No fumbling.

02

Open Chrome

Go to shopluminaryjewels.com/setup in Chrome or Edge. Click Connect. That's it.

03

Set Reminders

Pick your times and colors. Save to bracelet. The schedule lives inside the bracelet — no phone needed.

04

Wear & Glow

Unplug the cable and go. At reminder time your bracelet glows and vibrates. Double-tap to dismiss.
