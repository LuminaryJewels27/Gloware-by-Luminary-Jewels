Set Up Your Bracelet

Plug your bracelet into your computer with a USB-C cable, then connect below. Your reminders are saved onto the bracelet itself — unplug and they travel with you.

This browser can't connect to your bracelet.
Web Serial only works in Chrome, Edge, or Opera on a desktop computer or Android phone. Safari and Firefox don't support it, and it doesn't work on iPhone or iPad. If you're on a Mac, open this page in Chrome.

1. Connect

Plug the USB-C cable into your bracelet and into your computer, then click Connect. Your browser will ask you to choose the device — pick the one that appears.

2. Your Reminders

Pick a time, a colour, and how you'd like it to feel. Tap the circle to change colour.

About the colours: the nine shown are chosen because they stay clearly different from each other on the bracelet. You can pick any colour you like using the wheel, but pale shades — peach, blush, sage — come out looking close to white once they're glowing through the face, which makes reminders hard to tell apart at a glance.

3. Save to Bracelet

This replaces everything currently on the bracelet with the list above.
