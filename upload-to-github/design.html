Choose your metal

The setting your resin face sits in. All four are plated over the same steel base — the steel is what holds the face on magnetically, so the colour is purely yours to pick.

4

Choose your background

The colour poured behind everything else. Mica powders give a pearly shimmer; alcohol inks give clear, saturated colour.

Worth knowing before you pick. Your reminder light shines up through this layer, so the background tints it. Pale and pearly backgrounds let the colours come through true — a green reminder still reads green. Deep colours look beautiful in daylight but filter the light: a strong blue background makes every reminder look blue-ish, which is exactly what you don't want when you're telling them apart at a glance. Each option below says how much light it lets through.

Make it personal

Optional. The name costs nothing.

Add a name or a short word Set into the resin in fine gold or silver lettering. A name, a date, an initial — up to 14 characters. Included at no extra cost.
Add your own photo A pet, a place, a person. Your photo is printed and laminated before it goes into the resin — the lamination is what stops it yellowing or bleeding, and it's why this one is $1.

Your photo stays on your own device — the page reads it to show you the preview and nothing is uploaded anywhere. After you order we'll reply asking you to send the original, which is the copy we print from.

Please only send photos you took or have permission to use. We can't print copyrighted images — characters, album art, professional portraits — and a photographer usually holds the rights to a portrait even when you're the one in it.
