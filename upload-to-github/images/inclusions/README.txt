Photos of the glitters, botanicals and themed elements.

Name each file after the option's id — the exact filename for every one is
listed in photo-checklist.html, which you can just open in a browser. It
also shows you which ones are still missing.

.jpg, .jpeg, .png and .webp all work. Square, around 800x800.

Photos stay hidden on the shop until you open design.html and change
    const AUTO_IMAGES = false;
to
    const AUTO_IMAGES = true;

Anything without a photo keeps its coloured tile, so you can turn the
switch on early and let the page fill in as you shoot.
