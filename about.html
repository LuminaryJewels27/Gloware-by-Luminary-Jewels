We believe reminders
shouldn't interrupt —
they should inspire.

Luminary Jewels is a student-founded startup based in Rochester, New York. We create smart jewelry bracelets that glow.

We built Luminary Jewels because we were tired of being that person whose phone goes off at the wrong moment. We wanted something beautiful enough to wear as jewelry and smart enough to keep us on track.

Our bracelet delivers color-coded reminders through gentle LED light and vibration — no phone screen, no loud alarm, no interruption. Just a soft glow on your wrist that whispers instead of shouts.

We design for people who want technology that works quietly in the background. Not technology that demands your attention. Not another screen to check. Something that becomes part of your day so naturally you forget it's there — until you need it.

Every resin face is hand-poured in Rochester. Every bracelet is assembled with care. We're small on purpose — because small means personal, and personal means better.

Reminders, Reimagined.
